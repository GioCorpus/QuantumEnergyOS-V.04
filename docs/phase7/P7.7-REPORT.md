## 1. Executive Summary

P7.7 adds fleet-identity/trust modeling, distributed telemetry with correlation
IDs, classified energy telemetry with energy-aware scheduling, integrity-
protected checkpointing, and fleet failure simulation/recovery verification to
`qeos-cluster`. No sensor reading is fabricated; every energy figure is
classified.

**Baseline (P7.6):** 722 tests. **After P7.7:** 741 tests (+19), clean compile,
clippy-clean, zero unsafe.

---

## 2. P7.7-00/01 — Fleet Identity & Trust Boundaries

`crates/qeos-cluster/src/trust.rs`:
- `TrustModel` models explicit directional trust edges between Users, Nodes, the
  ControlPlane, Services, Devices and RemoteClusters — least privilege by
  default (User→Node is not trusted; only via the control plane).
- Authentication/authorization/rotation/revocation/audit are provided by
  `identity-service` (JWT/RBAC/audit) which is already in the workspace.

**REAL.** Tests: 4.

---

## 3. P7.7-02 — Distributed Telemetry

`crates/qeos-cluster/src/telemetry.rs`:
- `TraceContext` carries correlation IDs: trace_id, request_id, job_id,
  node_id, device_id, service_id, experiment_id.
- `TelemetryCollector` is a **bounded** collector (metrics, events, resource
  utilization); buffers stay capped while cumulative counters grow unbounded —
  supporting long-running fleets without unbounded growth (P7.7-05).

**REAL.** Tests: 3.

---

## 4. P7.7-03 — Energy Telemetry

`crates/qeos-cluster/src/energy.rs`:
- Every reading is classified `EnergySource::{Measured, Estimated, Simulated, Unavailable}`
  (default Unavailable). No fabricated watts/joules.
- `EnergyReading { source, power_watts, energy_joules, temperature_celsius }`.

**REAL** (classification). Tests: 5.

---

## 5. P7.7-04 — Energy-Aware Scheduling

`energy::evaluate(policy, reading)` honors `power_limit`, `energy_budget`,
`thermal_limit` and `battery_limit`. A constraint that cannot be verified
(unmeasured) is **denied**, never assumed. No global-optimality claim is made.

**REAL.** Tests included in the energy module.

---

## 6. P7.7-05 — Long-Running Workloads

Bounded telemetry buffers (P7.7-02) prevent telemetry/log growth on long runs.
Job/scheduler state machines are deterministic and terminal-state-fixed, so
queue/deadlock growth is bounded. Stress/long-run soak is validated in P7.9.

---

## 7. P7.7-06 — Checkpointing

`crates/qeos-cluster/src/checkpoint.rs`:
- `Checkpoint { format_version, job_id, data, checksum }` with a SHA-256
  integrity checksum.
- `verify_integrity()` detects tampering; `load(supported_version)` rejects
  incompatible (version-mismatched) checkpoints.
- Supports checkpoint/resume/retry/partial-recovery.

**REAL.** Tests: 4.

---

## 8. P7.7-07 — Fleet Failure Testing

`crates/qeos-cluster/src/fleet.rs`:
- `run_fleet_failure_matrix()` injects 7 faults — NodeLoss, NetworkInterruption,
  ServiceCrash, GpuFailure, QpuBackendFailure, StorageFailure,
  CredentialExpiration — and verifies detection + recovery for each.
- Fault *injection* is **SIMULATED**; the detection/recovery logic exercised
  (heartbeat expiry, offline/re-register, credential checks, drain) is **REAL**.

**Tests:** 3 (matrix asserts all 7 faults recover).
